Bigfoot

The Accidental Influencer

He wasn't trying to go viral. He wasn't trying to do anything, really. That's the whole point. Bigfoot showed up — clumsy, genuine, slightly confused by modern culture — and people couldn't look away. The brand doesn't need to try hard when the character already is who people want to be around.

Voice & Personality

He doesn't know
he's the story.

Tone
Unintentionally funny. Genuinely curious. Never trying. The humor comes from sincerity, not jokes.
Register
Conversational and slightly bewildered. Speaks like someone who just discovered office culture and finds it fascinating.
Signature move
Accidentally says the most profound thing in the room while reaching for a snack.
Avoids
Hype. Hard sells. Anything that feels like a performance. Bigfoot is always just… there.
World
Functional beverage brand. The product fits his vibe — something you reach for without overthinking it.
